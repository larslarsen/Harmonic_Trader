## Objective

Implement an immutable, dependency-light `Decimal` reconciliation oracle for linear
perpetual fixture events. Prove against deterministic NautilusTrader 2.0.0rc3 backtests
that causal submission/fill clocks, bid/ask spread, one-tick adverse slippage, taker fees,

The Nautilus engine is the simulated venue under test. It is not the accounting oracle.
The oracle must reconstruct economics from exact fixture primitives and normalized event
facts without using Nautilus position arithmetic as an input.

## Reviewer-Verified rc3 Semantics

The contract below is based on an in-memory reviewer probe against the exact provisioned
rc3 wheel:

- backtest processing first applies a market-data event to the simulated exchange, then
  invokes subscribed strategy callbacks, then settles eligible venue commands;
- a positive `StaticLatencyModel` holds a newly submitted command until a later engine
  clock reaches its venue-arrival time;
- `OneTickSlippageFillModel` fills a market buy one price increment above the current ask
  and a market sell one increment below the current bid;
- `MakerTakerFeeModel` charges the declared taker rate against actual fill notional;

  future timestamp and values the linear position from the current mark;

  `PositionAdjusted` event with adjustment type `FUNDING`; and
- the matching account balance change precedes portfolio processing of that adjustment.

not acceptance evidence for the developer drop.

## Required Independent Oracle

### 1. Immutable exact inputs

Define frozen, slotted records for a linear-perpetual ledger configuration and its ordered
fill, mark, and realized-funding inputs. Names may vary for clarity, but the records must
preserve at least:

- starting settlement cash, contract multiplier, price increment, taker-fee rate, and
  configured adverse-slippage tick count;
- a non-inverse linear-instrument declaration and settlement-currency precision;
- fill side, quantity, actual fill price, arrival bid and ask, actual commission,
  observation availability, economic decision, strictly positive computation latency,
  strategy submission, strictly positive venue latency, arrival-quote timestamp, and fill
  timestamp;
- mark timestamp and exact mark price; and
- funding publication timestamp, effective timestamp, exact rate, exact settlement mark,
  and the observed native funding cashflow when one exists.

All monetary, quantity, price, rate, and multiplier values must be finite `Decimal`
instances. Do not accept or coerce binary floats, booleans, strings, or integers as
numeric values. All clocks and durations must be non-boolean unsigned-64-bit integers.
Records and returned collections must be immutable.

Reject an inverse instrument, nonpositive multiplier/increment/quantity/price, negative
fee rate or commission, crossed or empty quotes, an actual fill on the improving side of
the declared arrival quote, inconsistent settlement currency/precision, timestamps out
of order, duplicate event identities, invalid sides/types, and nonfinite arithmetic.

Fixture fee and funding products are deliberately exact at eight settlement-currency
decimal places. Verify exact equality; do not invent a rounding policy or use a tolerance.

### 2. Causal execution trace

Validate every fill against this clock chain:

```text
observation availability <= economic decision
economic decision + strictly positive computation latency <= strategy submission
strategy submission + strictly positive venue latency <= fill
arrival quote timestamp == fill timestamp
```

Checked addition must reject unsigned-64-bit overflow. Keep observation availability,
decision, strategy submission, venue arrival/fill, funding publication, funding effective
time, and mark time as separate facts.

The test-only strategy must call the already accepted
`require_submission_after_latency` guard immediately before each order submission. A
Nautilus `OrderSubmitted` timestamp is the strategy-to-execution submission time; the
later `OrderFilled` timestamp is the venue result after `StaticLatencyModel`. Do not
rename venue arrival as strategy submission or treat transport latency as computation
latency.

Funding must require:

```text
funding publication < funding effective time
latest declared mark time <= funding effective time
```

Funding changes cash only when a nonzero position is held across the effective timestamp.
An indicative rate or a realized rate received after its effective time is not an
acceptable substitute fixture.

### 3. Linear netting ledger

Process normalized events in strict timestamp order using `Decimal` only. Track an actual
fill-price book and a parallel reference-mid book under NETTING semantics. Support at
least flat-to-open, same-side addition, partial reduction, complete close, and reversal
for both long and short positions.

For signed open quantity `q`, average price `a`, an opposite-side fill at price `p`,
closed quantity `c`, and positive multiplier `m`, realized price P&L is:

```text
sign(q) * (p - a) * c * m
```

Same-side additions use quantity-weighted average price. A partial reduction preserves
the remaining open average. A complete close clears it. A reversal realizes the closing
portion and opens the residual at the reversal fill price.

For each event, return an immutable snapshot containing at least:

- signed position and actual/reference average open prices;
- cumulative actual-fill and reference-mid realized price P&L;
- current actual-fill and reference-mid unrealized price P&L when a mark is known;
- cumulative actual-fill turnover;
- cumulative spread cost, adverse slippage cost, commissions, and signed funding
  cashflow;
- settlement cash; and
- net equity when valuation is available.

An open position without an explicit mark has unavailable unrealized P&L and net equity.
It must never be valued as zero or silently valued from a future mark.

### 4. Independent identities

For each fill, use the declared arrival bid/ask and their exact midpoint. Verify the
configured adverse-slip count against the actual native fill:

```text
buy fill  = ask + price_increment * adverse_slippage_ticks
sell fill = bid - price_increment * adverse_slippage_ticks
```

For this ticket's engine fixtures `adverse_slippage_ticks == 1`. Accumulate half-spread
and additional adverse slippage separately, with multiplier and absolute filled quantity
applied exactly once.

Verify commission independently:

```text
commission = abs(quantity) * actual_fill_price * multiplier * taker_fee_rate
```

For a nonzero signed position at realized funding time, independently compute signed
funding cashflow:

```text
funding_cashflow = -signed_position * settlement_mark * multiplier * funding_rate
```

Positive cashflow is a credit and negative cashflow is a debit. A flat position has zero
funding cashflow and must correspond to no native funding adjustment.

At every valued snapshot enforce both identities exactly:

```text
reference_mid_gross_pnl
    = actual_fill_gross_pnl + spread_cost + adverse_slippage_cost

net_pnl
    = actual_fill_gross_pnl - commissions + funding_cashflow
    = reference_mid_gross_pnl
      - spread_cost
      - adverse_slippage_cost
      - commissions
      + funding_cashflow

net_equity = starting_cash + net_pnl = settlement_cash + unrealized_actual_fill_pnl
```

Here gross P&L is cumulative realized price P&L plus current unrealized price P&L.
Spread and slippage therefore must not be subtracted a second time from actual-fill gross
P&L. Turnover is the sum of absolute actual fill notionals and is not P&L.

The final report must expose an immutable ordered snapshot tuple and exact cumulative
totals. Repeated reconciliation of equal independent inputs must compare equal and must
not mutate either the inputs or earlier snapshots.
